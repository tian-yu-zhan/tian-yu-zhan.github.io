Tianyu is the Director of Strategic Capability, Data and Statistical Sciences (DSS) at [AbbVie](https://www.abbvie.com/). He works closely with the executive leadership of Data and Statistical Sciences (DSS) to ensure governance strategies are aligned. Tianyu has delivered business impact at AbbVie, and was honored with three President Awards, one Development Award and an Early Career Research Award. As a dedicated mentor and strategic thinker, he leads critical company-wide and department-wide initiatives to elevate scientific standards. He has published 30 peer-reviewed articles (of which 19 statistical articles), two book chapters, and one monograph on deep-learning-assisted statistical methods. Tianyu also serves as an Associate Editor for four statistical journals (Statistics in Medicine, Statistics in Biopharmaceutical Research, Pharmaceutical Statistics, and Statistics in Biosciences), and was AE for Journal of Biopharmaceutical Statistics from 2022 to 2026. Tianyu is a co-founder and co-lead of the American Statistical Association (ASA) Biopharmaceutical Section (BIOP) Scientific Working Group [COMPASS (Computational, Objective-driven, and Meta Platform for Advanced Statistical Science)](https://asa-biop-compass.github.io/) that advocates for a broader application of computational methods and to collaborate with some statistical journals to encourage reproducible research. He is also a committee member and mentor of the ASA BIOP Mentoring Program [BRIDGE (Biopharmaceutical Resources for Industry Development, Growth, and Education)](https://bridgebiop.github.io/), which connects junior and senior statisticians.
